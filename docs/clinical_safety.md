# Clinical Safety and Limitations

CardioSleep Twin is a research prototype for clinical decision support. It does
not diagnose hypertension, replace validated BP measurement, prescribe
medication, or provide autonomous treatment recommendations. All patient data
used in this prototype is synthetic or simulated. Any clinical deployment would
require regulatory, clinical, privacy, security, and prospective validation.

- Wearable sleep and HRV data are contextual risk signals, not BP measurements.
- Home BP cuff readings are the reference measurements.
- Model outputs are probabilities, not deterministic diagnoses.
- The What-If Simulator shows associations learned from synthetic data, not
  guaranteed causal treatment effects.
